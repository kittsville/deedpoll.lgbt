What should I do once I've changed my name?

Once you've changed your name you should start telling organisations to update their records with your new name. It's easiest to get organisations to comply if you have legal ID in your new name.

  1. Take a photo of your signed deed poll. Keep the photo safe and accessible via your phone
  2. Update your driving licence
  3. Update your bank details
  4. Download or print a bank statement in your new name
  5. Update your passport using your deed poll and bank statement
  6. Contact everyone else:
    • Utility Companies
    • Creditors, if you have any loans or credit cards
    • The police, if you have a criminal record
    • Your employer
    • HMRC. Your employer will handle this if you are PAYE
    • Loyalty Cards

If relevant, consider updating the gender marker on your passport at the same time. This will save you the cost of updating your passport twice. You can get a gender change letter from your GP, an NHS gender clinic or a private gender clinic.
